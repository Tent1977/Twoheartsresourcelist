RENOUTOPIA

Start at Safe Camp. Fight through the showers, the laundry, the big tent, the cafeteria and the admin offices to reach the front door. The basket of bananas never runs out.

On iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen to install.

BEST: 0 points

A parody. Every character is a fictional stick figure. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or depicting any real person, agency, county, or nonprofit.

PICK YOUR RESIDENT

HOW TO PLAY

  • 🍌 Every banana that hits a staff member = 1 point. Knocking one out earns a bonus.
  • ⭐ Every 5 points another resident joins your crew and throws bananas with you.
  • 🚪 Clear each zone to unlock the door. Seven zones from Safe Camp to the front entrance.
  • 👑 The Big Kahuna guards the exit. If you don't have 5 people throwing bananas, he wins.
  • ❤️ You get 3 lives. Crew members who take too many hits get trespassed.
  • 💩 The shower puddles slow you down.

THE CHAIN OF COMMAND

  1. Facility Assistant — swings a mop or broom
  2. Maintenance — hurls wrenches
  3. Caseworker — non-stop notebooks
  4. Supervisor — points a finger; beebees fly, some catch fire
  5. Assistant Administrator — smacks a ruler, then smacks you
  6. Administrator — staplers and pens
  7. THE BIG KAHUNA — all of the above

CONTROLS

Computer: WASD / arrows to move · mouse to aim · click to throw · 1–4 or Q/E or scroll to switch weapons · Space or right-click = Ralph's fart · P = pause · M = mute

Phone: left thumb moves · right thumb aims and throws · buttons for weapons and the fart

PAUSED

THE BIG KAHUNA SAYS NO.

You lose a life and get escorted back to the Admin Offices. Rack up points — every 5 brings another banana thrower.

YOU GOT TRESPASSED

YOU MADE IT OUT THE FRONT DOOR

The Big Kahuna is a pile of ash. Welcome to Reno Utopia.
