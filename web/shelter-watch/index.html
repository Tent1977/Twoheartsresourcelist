About this site. Shelter Watch is an independent community project. The Advisory Board of Lived Experience (ABLE) serves as an advisory partner only — ABLE does not operate, inspect or rate any facility.

Affiliations, disclosed. People involved in this project are connected to organizations listed here, including Volunteers of America (Northern California & Northern Nevada) and RISE (Reno Initiative for Shelter and Equality), which is ABLE's fiscal sponsor. Ratings are calculated the same way for every facility, and the formula is shown on every page.

Research draft. Listings were compiled in November 2026 from public sources and many are not yet confirmed by phone. Always call before you go. In an emergency call 911; for shelter and services call 211.

Reviews are the opinions of the people who wrote them. Do not post phone numbers, emails, or anyone's private information. Report anything that crosses the line.
